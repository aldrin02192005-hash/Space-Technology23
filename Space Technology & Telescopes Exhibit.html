Diwata-1

First Filipino Microsatellite

~50 kgDeployed 27 Apr 2016Ended 2020

Carried to the International Space Station on a Cygnus cargo flight and released from the Japanese Kibo module on 27 April 2016.

  • High Precision Telescope (HPT)
  • Spaceborne Multispectral Imager
  • Wide and Middle Field Cameras
Used for weather monitoring, crop and forest observation, and disaster response imagery. Its orbit lasted almost four years, far beyond the 18 months planned.
Read: end of missionRead: UP Diliman story
Diwata-2

The Upgraded Successor

~50 kgLaunched 29 Oct 2018H-IIA rocket

Launched from Tanegashima, Japan, with improved cameras and an amateur radio payload that lets ham operators connect through the satellite.

  • Multispectral imager
  • Wide-field and enhanced-resolution cameras
  • Amateur radio repeater
By late 2022 it had taken over 112,000 images and covered about 94% of Philippine land, orbiting near 600 km altitude.

● Japan's Asteroid Explorers

JAXA's Hayabusa missions brought pieces of asteroids back to Earth.

Hayabusa

First Asteroid Sample Return

Launched May 2003Target: ItokawaReturned Jun 2010

Reached asteroid Itokawa in 2005 using ion engines and collected about 1,500 tiny grains despite many technical troubles. Its capsule landed in Woomera, Australia, in June 2010.

The first spacecraft to return material from an asteroid.
JAXA/ISAS missions
Hayabusa2

Ryugu and Beyond

Launched Dec 2014Target: RyuguReturned Dec 2020

Arrived at Ryugu in 2018, deployed small landers and rovers, created an artificial crater, and returned more than 5 g of carbon-rich samples. It continues on an extended mission to asteroid 1998 KY26.

Ryugu samples carry water-bearing minerals and organic molecules.

● Key Technologies

Ideas that made these missions possible.

Microsatellites

Small ~50 kg spacecraft that cost far less than traditional satellites, letting new space nations build their own.

Ion Propulsion

Electric thrusters give gentle, extremely efficient thrust. Hayabusa used them to travel to Itokawa and back.

Multispectral Imaging

Cameras capture many wavelengths to reveal plant health, water quality and clouds.

Sample-Return Capsules

Heat-shielded capsules survive re-entry to deliver samples intact for laboratory study.
